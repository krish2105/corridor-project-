Critical

A survey day that was never observed

$IDENT of $SERIES movement-class series reproduce the previous day to the exact vehicle while their 15-minute bins differ. Of those that move, $GREATER rise and $SMALLER fall.

Under independent counting that split has probability p ≈ 2×10−39. The authority paid for two days and holds one.

Critical

The scheme's key movement, uncounted

JDA is converting this road to signal-free operation with seven U-turn bays. The survey commissioned for it contains no U-turn column anywhere.

The survey drawing shows $UTURNS median gaps wide enough for a vehicle to turn through. The demand exists and was never measured.

Material

Demand understated by at least $UPLIFT%

Static PCU factors applied against a composition-dependent standard. Two-wheelers are 49% of this stream and are carried at the IRC:106 value reserved for a class below 5%.

The defensible correction runs from +$UPLIFT% to +$BANDHI%. The spread is the cost of a class scheme that records half the stream in one column.

Critical

Reported classes do not match the data

The flow-diagram sheet carries a 20-class header over 10-class data, offset by one column. The two-wheeler count appears under "Taxi."

And what the data does support

The scheme case, quantified

An audit that only finds fault is not useful. Through movements are $THRULO–$THRUHI% of junction traffic. Carried over the junctions rather than through them, $CAPOK of $CAPN corridor approaches return under planning capacity — volume/capacity falls from $VCWAS to $VCLO–$VCHI.

That is the argument turning-movement data exists to make, and no other dataset makes it. It is also the number that decides whether an elevated corridor is justified or merely expensive.

Why this holds up

  • Never trust a stored total. Every subtotal recomputed from its components; disagreements registered, never absorbed.
  • State the assumption, band the answer. Where data cannot settle a question, the output is a range with the cause named — not a point estimate with false precision.
  • Test the model before quoting it. The capacity analysis reports that a lane-based v/c does not describe this corridor, rather than quoting a grade it cannot support.
  • Publish the error rate on the front page. MAPE, homography RMSE, georeference RMSE. Unusual in Indian traffic reporting, and deliberate.
  • Independence is structural. No fee contingent on a project proceeding. An assurance service paid on success is not one.

Stated limits

A capability statement that overclaims is worth less than one that does not.

  • Delivery is solo, full-time. One corridor at a time, ten weeks end to end.
  • Single-camera capacity is one junction per peak. Full-corridor primary collection needs a partnered field team.
  • Partner-fronted: structural, geotechnical, environmental clearance, and any tender requiring empanelled principal status.
  • Out of scope: construction supervision, EPC, signal implementation.

The question to put to your own data

Not whether the survey looks professional. Whether anyone has recomputed a single total in it.

On this corridor the answer was no, and it took $BINS parsed bins to show that the authority had paid for two days of observation and received one. The same check applies to every survey feeding every scheme currently in DPR.
